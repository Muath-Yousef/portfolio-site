Engineering rules

Designed to fail safely

  • SOAR_DRY_RUN=true by default; sensitive actions require human approval.
  • CDN and RFC1918 addresses are never automatically blocked.
  • DNS events are NOTIFY_ONLY, never BLOCK_IP.
  • Raw client data is not sent to external AI providers.
  • Production claims require tests, operational evidence, and rollback paths.
